About us

A space to pause from life's daily noise

NaadLeela began with a simple belief: in a world that rarely slows down, we all need moments of stillness. Rooted in the experience of sound, our studio brings together singing bowls, gongs, and mindful practices to create space for rest, reflection, and self-connection

We offer individual sound healing sessions, other services and courses as well

The founder

Dr. Aditi Sunil Peshane

Sound Healing Practitioner

A Certified Physiotherapist with Tradtional Training in art of sound healing, Dr Aditi started Naad Leela. Through her work with sound healing, she aims to create thoughtful, welcoming experiences where people can pause, relax, and reconnect with themselves.

With NaadLeela, this vision takes shape in a dedicated space for individual sessions, group sound baths, and learning experiences for those who wish to explore sound more deeply.
