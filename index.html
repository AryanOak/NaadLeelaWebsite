The practice

What is sound healing?

Sound healing is a wellness practice that uses sound, vibration and mindful listening to create a calm and relaxing experience.

During a session, instruments such as singing bowls, gongs, chimes and tuning forks are played around you while you rest comfortably. The sounds and vibrations encourage you to slow down, focus your attention and take a break from the noise of everyday life.

You don't need to do anything—just lie down, listen and allow yourself to relax.

  • Singing bowlsHimalayan & crystal
  • GongsWind & symphonic
  • ChimesKoshi & bar chimes
  • Tuning forksWeighted & Otto
